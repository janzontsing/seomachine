## Internal Linking Best Practices

When adding internal links to DOMI blog content:

1. **Link to product pages from relevant application content**: An article about robot obstacle avoidance should link to the DMAS2M001 product page.
2. **Link to application pages from product content**: A DM0301 deep-dive should link to relevant application pages (smart home, liquid level sensing).
3. **Link to the Knowledge Hub from all articles**: Build topic cluster authority around the blog hub.
4. **3-5 strategic links per post**: Don't over-link. Each link should genuinely help the reader evaluate or learn more.
5. **Use descriptive, keyword-rich anchor text**: "DM0301 1D ToF sensor" not "this product."
6. **Deep-link to specific product pages**, not just category pages or the homepage.
7. **Cross-link between related blog articles**: A dToF vs iToF article should link to specific product deep-dives.
